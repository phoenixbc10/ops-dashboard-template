🎓 How the AI learns
Both assistants — the crew one and this dashboard's — answer from three sources, in this order:
  1. The Notion knowledge base — your Team Handbook and Pricing Guide pages. Edit those pages in Notion and the AI picks the changes up automatically (it re-syncs every 6 hours — or press Sync now after an edit).
  2. Your training notes (below) — facts and rules you type right here. These override the Notion pages when they conflict, and go live within a minute.
  3. The live job schedule — attached automatically so it knows what's booked. Not trainable.
✏️ Teach the AI
One clear fact or rule per note — prices, ratios, policies, wording. Example: “Solar panel cleans are $10 per panel, minimum $100.” The AI treats these as gospel, so keep them accurate — remove a note if it goes stale.
❓ Questions it couldn't answer
Flagged automatically when a fact wasn't in its knowledge. Press “Teach” to write the missing answer.

For scaling, watch profit per job after ads (gross profit per job minus what you paid to win it) and profit ROAS (gross profit / ad spend). ROAS on revenue alone can look great while margins bleed. Above 1.0 profit ROAS means each dollar of ads returns more than a dollar of gross profit. Results taper as you spend more, so raise budget gradually and watch cost per job.
CRM Hygiene · AI keeps your GoHighLevel tidy
This agent reads your GoHighLevel Conversations and quietly keeps the CRM accurate. For each active lead it fills in contact details the customer mentioned (street address, suburb, phone, email) and moves them to the right pipeline stage. It only ever fills blanks, never overwrites what you already have, and anything unclear is flagged for you instead of changed. It does not message customers.
